# Dune Skipper (`car_skipper`)

**Archetype:** open sand-rail buggy  
**Unlock:** Stage 2 (proposed)  
**Stats (proposal, 1 to 5):** Accel 5, Top speed 2, Handling 4 (half off-road penalty)  
**Default paint:** safety orange tube frame with sky-blue body panels  
**Design notes:** New in round 4. Original design, no real make or badge. A light, twitchy car for the Redrock and Harvest dirt edges. Exposed tube frame and paddle tires make it the most see-through silhouette in the game. Distinct features are spelled out and the prompt says what to avoid, because generators drift toward famous real cars (round 2 flagged five of the first eight). Check the hero against real cars before generating the other sheets.

How to generate: do 01 first. Once the hero looks original, give it to the generator as the **reference image** for 02 to 04 so the design stays on-model. The steering sheet is a design check only: generators can't hold seven consistent frames, so production frames come from the 3D render pipeline (docs/06-concept-review-round2.md).
