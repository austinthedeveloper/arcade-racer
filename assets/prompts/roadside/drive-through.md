# Drive-Through Pieces (road-spanning sprites)

**What these are:** sprites that span the whole road, so the car drives *through* them: tunnel mouths, bridges, arches, overpasses, gates. The renderer needs a new road-spanning sprite type for these (Neon tunnel and Timberline covered bridge are on hold for it). The roadside sheets drew some of these as small side props seen at an angle; these prompts ask for the straight-on, symmetrical version the renderer needs.

Every prompt asks for:
- **straight-on front view, perfectly symmetrical left to right**, as seen by a driver approaching in the middle of the road;
- **the opening filled with flat solid magenta #FF00FF** so it keys out and the road shows through;
- an opening about **2.5 times wider than tall** (a four-lane road under it), with the side pillars or walls standing just outside the opening;
- one piece per image so it comes out large and clean.

Each place has an **entrance** (seen from outside). Tunnels and covered bridges also need an **interior frame**, a ring the renderer repeats every few road segments while the car is inside, which sells the speed.

Common suffix for every prompt below: `straight-on front view, perfectly symmetrical, the opening is filled with flat solid magenta #FF00FF, isolated on a flat solid magenta #FF00FF background, no ground shadow, no road, no cars, no readable text`.
